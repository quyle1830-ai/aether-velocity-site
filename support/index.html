Support

Need help with Aether Velocity? Email quyle.jp@gmail.com. Please include your iPhone model, iOS version and what happened.

Restore purchases on a new device

Sign in with the same Apple Account, open the Garage (sparkle icon at the top), go to WALLET and tap RESTORE PURCHASES. Cars, worlds and an active Ad-Free Pass come back.

Ad-Free Pass

The Ad-Free Pass is an auto-renewing subscription, weekly or monthly. It removes ads between flights and lets you revive without watching an ad. It renews automatically unless cancelled at least 24 hours before the current period ends.

To cancel: open Settings on your iPhone, tap your name, then Subscriptions, or tap MANAGE SUBSCRIPTION in the game's Wallet screen.

Refunds

Purchases are made through Apple, so refunds are requested from Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com. A refunded item is removed from the game.

Diamonds and progress

Diamonds, best scores and unlocked items are saved on your device. Diamonds cannot be bought with money and are not transferred between devices in this version. Deleting the game deletes them; App Store purchases can always be restored.

Ad privacy choices

Where it applies, use the privacy button in the Garage to change your ad consent. See the Privacy Policy.
