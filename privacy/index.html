Privacy Policy

Aether Velocity · Effective date: November 8, 2026

Aether Velocity ("the game") is made by 1988studio ("we"). This policy explains what information the game handles and why. Contact us at quyle.jp@gmail.com.

Summary

  • The game has no accounts and no sign-in. We do not run servers that receive your data.
  • Your progress stays on your device.
  • Purchases are handled by Apple. We never see your payment details.
  • The free version shows ads from Google AdMob. Google may collect device and usage information to show and measure those ads, as described below.

Information stored on your device

The game saves the following on your device only: diamond wallet, unlocked cars and worlds, best scores, settings (sound, vibration), which tips you have seen, and a cached copy of your App Store purchase status. We do not receive this information. Deleting the game deletes it.

Purchases

In-app purchases and the Ad-Free Pass subscription are processed by Apple under your Apple Account. The game checks on your device which items your Apple Account owns, so it can unlock them and restore them on a new device. We do not receive your name, email address or payment information. Apple's privacy policy applies to the purchase itself: apple.com/legal/privacy.

You can manage or cancel the Ad-Free Pass in your Apple Account subscription settings, or from the game's Wallet screen.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

Unless you have an active Ad-Free Pass, the game shows ads between some flights and offers optional ads that let you continue a flight. The ads are provided by Google AdMob, and the consent message is provided by Google's User Messaging Platform.

The game requests non-personalized ads and does not ask for permission to track you across other companies' apps (it shows no App Tracking Transparency prompt). To deliver, limit and measure ads and to prevent fraud, Google's software may still collect information such as:

  • device identifiers that are limited to this app (not the advertising ID);
  • IP address and the approximate location derived from it;
  • ad interactions, such as views and taps;
  • performance and crash diagnostics.

In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland, and in other regions where it is required, Google's consent message is shown before ads load. You can change your choice at any time with the privacy button in the Garage. Google's use of this information is described at policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

Photos and sharing

When you share a score card, the system share sheet opens and you choose where it goes. The game asks for permission to add a photo only if you choose Save Image. It cannot read your photo library.

Children

The game is not directed to children under 13, or under the minimum age set by the law of your country. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. Parents can turn off in-app purchases with Screen Time.

Keeping and deleting information

We hold no personal information about you. To remove the game's data, delete the game from your device. To manage the information Google collects for ads, see the Google link above or use the privacy button in the Garage.

Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR, the UK GDPR or US state privacy laws), you may have rights to access, delete or object to the processing of personal information. Because we do not hold your information, most requests concern Apple or Google. Contact us and we will help you reach the right place.

Changes

If the game starts handling information differently, for example by adding Game Center leaderboards or iCloud saves, we will update this page and the effective date before that version is released.
